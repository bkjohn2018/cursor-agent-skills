# Data Governance

## When to Use
- Launching or re-structuring a data governance program.
- Defining governance councils, decision rights, and policy lifecycle.
- Formalizing business glossary, standards, issue management, and compliance.

## DMBOK Governance Activities
Anchor plans to these core activities:
- Perform readiness assessment and business alignment.
- Define governance strategy and operating framework.
- Develop goals, principles, policies, and standards.
- Engage issue management and regulatory compliance.
- Sponsor glossary, procedures, and cross-functional touchpoints.
- Embed governance through change management and measurement.

## Workflow
1. Clarify scope, business drivers, and governance outcomes.
2. Assess readiness across sponsorship, roles, process, tooling, and culture.
3. Define operating model (council charters, RACI, decision rights, escalation).
4. Establish policy and standards lifecycle (draft, review, approve, enforce).
5. Stand up glossary governance, issue triage, and compliance tracking.
6. Define scorecards and adoption metrics; run periodic maturity review.

## Quality Checklist
- Governance scope is explicit and bounded.
- Decision rights and accountability are clear.
- Policies, glossary, and issue workflows are integrated.
- Metrics track both adoption and risk reduction.
