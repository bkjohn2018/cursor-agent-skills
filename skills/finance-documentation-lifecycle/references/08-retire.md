# Phase 8: Retire – Act

**Goal**: Discontinue when no longer needed. Archive with retirement date and reason; retain per policy; dispose per retention schedule.

**ISO 9001 alignment**: Clause 7.5.3.2 requires organizations to manage "retention and disposition" of documented information. Retired documentation must be kept as evidence (if applicable) or properly disposed.

---

## Steps

### 1. Identify Retirement Need

**Triggers for retirement**:

| Trigger | Example | Retention Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Process discontinued** | Department merged; function outsourced | Archive; retain 3–7 years per policy |
| **Replaced by new procedure** | Process redesigned; new SOP released | Archive superseded version; retain per policy |
| **Regulation changed** | Compliance requirement no longer applies | Archive; clarify with compliance what retention is needed |
| **System retired** | Legacy system decommissioned; new system deployed | Archive; remove if system-specific (screenshots, field names, etc.) |
| **Merge with other docs** | GL reconciliation merged into comprehensive "Period Close" procedure | Archive separately; cross-reference new procedure |
| **No longer used** | Procedure for manual task that's now automated | Archive; confirm with owner no users depend on it |

**Document retirement decision**:
```
RETIREMENT NOTICE
─────────────────────────────────────────
Document Title:       Manual Invoice Matching Procedure
Current Version:      1.2 (dated May 2024)
Retirement Date:      September 30, 2025
Reason:               Process automated; new system deployed (Coupa)
Replaced By:          [Link to new system documentation]
Retirement Authority: Finance Manager (John Lee)
Retention Required:   YES (7 years for SOX compliance)
Archive Location:     [Path/Link]
Decision Evidence:    [Approval email / workflow ID / meeting record]
─────────────────────────────────────────
```

---

### 2. Assess Retention Requirement

**Determine how long to keep**:

| Document Type | Typical Retention | Regulation |
|---|---|---|
| **Control procedures** | 7 years | SOX, tax |
| **Reconciliation procedures** | 7 years | SOX, tax, audit |
| **Financial reporting guides** | 7 years | SOX, regulatory |
| **Non-control reference docs** | 3 years | General practice |
| **Training materials** | 3 years | Operational |

**Check retention policy**:
- Does your organization have a document retention policy? (Legal, Compliance, Records Management can advise)
- Are there regulatory requirements? (SOX, tax, industry-specific)
- Does this document provide evidence of controls or compliance? (If yes, keep longer)

**Document retention decision**:
```
RETENTION DETERMINATION
─────────────────────────────────────────
Document:            Manual Invoice Matching Procedure
Retirement Date:     September 30, 2025
Retention Required:  YES
Retention Period:    7 years (until September 30, 2032)
Reason:              Provides evidence of prior control design
                     and operation; required for SOX audit trail
Disposal Date:       October 1, 2032
Disposal Method:     Secure deletion (digital) or shredding (printed)
─────────────────────────────────────────
```

---

### 3. Communicate Retirement

**Notify users and stakeholders**:
- Finance team: "This procedure is being retired; effective [date]"
- New procedure (if applicable): "Use [new procedure] instead"
- Transition date: When to stop using old procedure / start using new
- Owner contact: Who to ask if questions about old procedure
- Controlled copy status: authoritative copy will move to archive and is no longer valid for active use
- Uncontrolled copies: printed, emailed, downloaded, or local copies must be removed, marked retired, or replaced with the successor link

**Example retirement notice**:
```
PROCEDURE RETIRED: Manual Invoice Matching Procedure v1.2

Effective Retirement Date: September 30, 2025

REASON:
Invoice matching process has been automated and migrated to Coupa.

WHAT TO DO:
- STOP using this manual procedure effective September 30, 2025
- START using new Coupa Invoice Matching Guide (released Sept 1)
- TRAINING: Mandatory Coupa training sessions [dates/links]

QUESTIONS?
- Coupa-specific: Contact system owner [name, email]
- About old procedure: Contact John Lee (Finance Manager)

ARCHIVAL:
Old procedure will be archived for audit trail and compliance retention.
```

---

### 4. Archive Documentation

**How to archive**:

**Digital**:
- Move to archive folder or storage location (e.g., "Sharepoint/Archive/" or "Retired Procedures/")
- Filename clearly marked: "RETIRED_Manual_Invoice_Matching_v1.2_2025-09-30"
- Metadata header updated with retirement date and reason
- Still accessible for audit purposes, but flagged as "RETIRED"

**Printed**:
- If printed versions exist, collect them (or ask document owner to do so)
- Mark "RETIRED [Date]" on cover page
- Store in secure, climate-controlled location
- Index showing what was archived, when, why

**Uncontrolled copies**:
- Ask document owners and managers to remove known unofficial copies from shared drives, emails, local folders, and training packets where practical.
- If removal is not practical, issue a notice that uncontrolled copies are invalid and users must use the successor or archive link.
- Retain evidence of the retirement notice and any exception allowing temporary use during transition.

**Cross-reference in new procedure**:
- If replaced by new procedure: "This procedure supersedes Manual Invoice Matching v1.2 (retired Sept 30, 2025)"
- Link to archived version in case audit trail needed

---

### 5. Update Master Documentation Registry

**Remove from active list** (or mark "retired"):
```
FINANCE DOCUMENTATION REGISTRY (Updated Oct 1, 2025)
─────────────────────────────────────────
Document Title          | Version | Owner  | Status    | Archive Date
GL Reconciliation SOP   | 2.0     | Jane   | APPROVED  | —
Close Checklist         | 1.1     | John   | APPROVED  | —
Coupa Invoice Matching  | 1.0     | Jane   | APPROVED  | —
Manual Invoice Matching | 1.2     | Jane   | RETIRED   | 2025-09-30  ← Archived
─────────────────────────────────────────
```

**Update indexes and process maps**:
- Remove retired procedure from active procedure index
- Update process flow diagrams: show old procedure retired, new procedure in effect
- Update linked documents: change cross-references to new procedure

---

### 6. Maintain Audit Trail

**Retain retirement documentation**:
```
RETIREMENT RECORD (for audit trail)
─────────────────────────────────────────
Retired Document:      Manual Invoice Matching Procedure v1.2
Retirement Date:       September 30, 2025
Retirement Authority:  Finance Manager (John Lee)
Reason for Retirement: Process automated; migrated to Coupa
Successor:             Coupa Invoice Matching Guide v1.0
Archive Location:      [Sharepoint/Archive/Retired_Procedures/]
Retention Until:       September 30, 2032 (7 years for SOX)
Disposal Method:       Secure deletion
Archive Marker:        RETIRED_Manual_Invoice_Matching_v1.2_2025-09-30
─────────────────────────────────────────
```

**Evidence of decision**:
- Email or memo authorizing retirement (Finance Manager, CFO, Compliance)
- Approval date
- Business justification
- Successor document approval, if replaced
- Exception decisions for transition use, if any
- Confirmation that the registry, indexes, and related references were updated

---

## Retirement Checklist

Before finalizing retirement, confirm:

- [ ] Retirement need documented (process change, automation, merger, etc.)
- [ ] Retention requirement assessed (SOX? Tax? 3–7 years?)
- [ ] Successor procedure (if any) identified and released
- [ ] Finance team notified of retirement and effective date
- [ ] Training or transition support provided (if needed)
- [ ] Active documentation registry updated
- [ ] Related procedures and indexes updated (old procedure removed/linked to new)
- [ ] Old procedure archived with clear "RETIRED" marker
- [ ] Known uncontrolled copies removed, marked retired, or replaced where practical
- [ ] Retirement record created (for audit trail)
- [ ] Retention until date documented (for future disposal)
- [ ] Disposal method defined (secure deletion, shredding, etc.)
- [ ] Retirement approval, notification, archive, exception, and disposal evidence retained

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Why It Matters | How to Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Retired doc not archived; old version still circulates | Users follow outdated procedure; audit gap | Mark "RETIRED" clearly; move to archive; notify users of effective date |
| Retention requirement not assessed | Document destroyed too soon; audit trail lost; compliance violation | Check SOX, tax, audit requirements; consult Legal/Compliance; retain 3–7 years minimum for controls |
| Users not notified | Confusion; some use old, some use new; process breaks | Send email notification with effective date, new procedure link, training info |
| Archive location unclear; document lost | Can't retrieve if audit needs historical evidence | Maintain archive folder with clear naming; keep master list of what's archived |
| Successor procedure not ready | Users have no guidance after old procedure retired | Release new procedure before retiring old; allow transition period (2–4 weeks) |
| Retirement done informally | No evidence retirement was authorized; audit finding | Get approval from Finance Manager/CFO; document decision in writing |

---

## Output: Archived and Retained Documentation

By the end of this phase, you should have:

1. **Retirement decision documented** (reason, authority, date)
2. **Retention requirement determined** (per regulatory/policy requirements)
3. **Retired procedure archived** clearly marked with retirement date
4. **Finance team notified** of retirement, effective date, and successor (if applicable)
5. **Master registry updated** (old procedure removed or marked retired)
6. **Uncontrolled copy handling completed** (removed, marked retired, replaced, or exception retained)
7. **Retention record maintained** (until date, disposal method, archive location)

## Lifecycle Complete

Congratulations! You've guided documentation through its complete lifecycle:

1. ✅ **Prepare** – Defined purpose, owner, audience, compliance drivers
2. ✅ **Structure** – Organized content, established metadata, formatting
3. ✅ **Review** – Verified accuracy, completeness, clarity, control design
4. ✅ **Approve** – Obtained formal sign-off from authorized managers
5. ✅ **Publish/Store** – Placed in authoritative location with access control
6. ✅ **Use** – Monitored usage, feedback, deviations, control effectiveness
7. ✅ **Revise** – Updated when processes changed, gaps identified, feedback incorporated
8. ✅ **Retire** – Discontinued, archived, retained per policy, audit trail maintained

**Result**: Your finance and accounting team has accurate, current, usable, and auditable documentation that supports their work and demonstrates control design to auditors.

---

## Key Principle

**Documentation is part of your process, not separate from it.** When documentation is weak, the process breaks. When documentation is strong, the process is repeatable, controllable, and auditable. Invest in it.
