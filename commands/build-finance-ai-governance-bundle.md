Build a controlled, testable, auditable AI governance package for finance and accounting use of AI tools, assistants, models, automations, vendor AI, and agents.

Use this command when the user wants a finance/accounting AI governance package, not just a single AI policy or readiness checklist. The package should connect safe-use boundaries, use-case intake, risk/control mapping, agent readiness, data protection, documentation lifecycle, and stakeholder communication.

Collect these inputs first:
- Finance/accounting scope, business function, and AI governance objective
- AI use cases, tools, vendors, models, assistants, automations, or agents in scope
- Target audience, sponsor, accountable owner, reviewers, approvers, and exception authority
- Finance processes affected: close, reporting, reconciliations, AP, AR, payroll, tax, treasury, audit, FP&A, controls, or management reporting
- Data involved: GL, vendor, customer, employee, payroll, tax, bank, contract, forecast, audit, executive, PII, regulated, or unreleased financial data
- Current policy, standards, documentation, control, security/privacy, or AI guidance already available
- Known risks, incidents, exceptions, audit findings, control gaps, privacy/security concerns, or prohibited-use concerns
- Maturity level: draft, pilot, operational, formalized, audit-ready
- Required outputs and delivery format
- Desired tone: executive, operational, training-oriented, audit-ready
- Target effective date, review cadence, pilot/rollout timing, and recertification expectations

Execute by phase:

1. **Delivery framing**
   - Apply `governance-project-delivery`.
   - Define value statement, scope, stakeholders, milestones, acceptance criteria, risks, issues, change control, and closure criteria.

2. **AI safe-use foundation**
   - Apply `finance-ai-safe-use-policy`.
   - Define permitted, restricted, and prohibited uses; approval tiers; user obligations; human accountability; disclosure; evidence; exceptions; incidents; and review cadence.
   - Apply `policy-and-standard-writing` only after requirements are approved and formal policy wording is needed.

3. **AI use-case intake**
   - Apply `finance-ai-use-case-intake`.
   - Create intake requirements for proposed AI use, including business purpose, tool/vendor, AI capability, data sensitivity, process impact, risk tier, and routing decisions.

4. **AI risk and control mapping**
   - Apply `finance-ai-risk-control-mapping`.
   - Map AI risks to control objectives, controls, owners, reviewers, frequency, evidence, residual risk, approval conditions, monitoring, and recertification.
   - Apply `internal-control-design` for detailed control design, testing, deficiencies, and remediation.

5. **Agent readiness**
   - Apply `ai-agent-readiness-assessment` for any AI agent, tool access, workflow automation, system action, scheduled run, delegated decision, or partial autonomy.
   - Define autonomy boundaries, permissions, human approval gates, failure modes, testing, logging, rollback, monitoring, and readiness decision.

6. **Data security, privacy, and data trust**
   - Apply `data-security-and-privacy-controls` for sensitive data, PII, access, logging, encryption, external sharing, retention, and privacy/security exceptions.
   - Apply `data-standards-management` when AI requirements should become baseline standards.
   - Apply `metadata-and-lineage`, `data-quality-controls`, and `data-issue-management` when lineage, quality monitoring, or issue closure are required.

7. **Documentation and lifecycle**
   - Apply `governance-writing-style-guide` before drafting governed artifacts.
   - Apply `process-and-procedure-writing` or `sop-writing` for operating procedures, intake procedures, review procedures, or incident handling procedures.
   - Apply `finance-documentation-lifecycle` for controlled copy location, approval, publication, access, version history, retention, revision, retirement, and audit evidence.

8. **Communication and adoption**
   - Apply `executive-summary-writing` for leadership-ready summary.
   - Apply `governance-ppt-deck-writing` for committee, risk, audit, or executive deck outline.
   - Include adoption guidance, training notes, user obligations, and escalation paths.

Output must be coherent across:
- AI scope and out-of-scope boundaries
- Permitted, restricted, and prohibited use categories
- Intake questions, risk tiers, and approval routing
- Roles, RACI, owners, approvers, reviewers, and exception authority
- AI risks, control objectives, controls, evidence, testing, and residual risk
- Agent autonomy boundaries, permissions, approval gates, and readiness decisions
- Security/privacy requirements, data handling rules, and exceptions
- Monitoring, incidents, issue handling, recertification, and change triggers
- Documentation lifecycle metadata and governance narrative

Default output sections:
1. Executive Summary and Decision Request
2. AI Governance Charter / Delivery Plan
3. AI Safe Use Policy Requirements
4. Finance AI Use Case Intake Procedure
5. AI Risk Tiering and Approval Model
6. AI Risk and Control Matrix
7. AI Agent Readiness Assessment Template
8. Security and Privacy Control Assessment
9. Data Standards, Metadata, Lineage, and Quality Requirements
10. Exception, Incident, and Issue Handling Procedure
11. Monitoring and Recertification Plan
12. RACI / Roles and Responsibilities
13. Documentation Lifecycle Metadata
14. Adoption / Training Notes
15. Executive or Governance Deck Outline
16. Version History, Change Log, and Open Decisions
